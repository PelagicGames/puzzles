Light Discs

Tutorial

Learn how filters, patterns, rotation, and snapping combine to form each target.

01 / How to play

Build the target filters

1

Rotate

Use the curved arrows to rotate a free triangle clockwise or anticlockwise by 60 degrees. Patterns rotate with their triangle.

2

Drag and snap

Drag a triangle within the board so that one or more of its corner discs overlap white fixed discs. Drop it to snap coincident circles together.

3

Combine filters

Only RGB light channels present in every overlapping filter remain. White passes all light; black blocks all light.

4

Match the targets

Reproduce the three small target discs, including colours, shapes, and orientation. Visually equivalent filter combinations also match.

Success state

Success appears when each movable triangle has at least one corner circle snapped to a fixed circle and all three colour-and-shape formations match their targets. Random targets use at least five snapped circles, but the player's solution does not need to. Moving a triangle away hides success again.

02 / Colour reference

Every filter interaction

The lens-shaped overlap shows the resulting common RGB channels. The order of the two filters does not matter.

03 / Pattern reference

Every possible shape

A shape may use a different filter colour from its surrounding disc. Squares and triangles fit inside the large disc; the small disc fits inside both.
