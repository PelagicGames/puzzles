Level-code reference

Design a layout

Use four sections in this order: R:, G:, B:, and T:. Each section contains three components in top, left, bottom order.

A component starts with a shape, followed by its background colour and, for shaped discs, its shape colour. For example, twr is a red triangle on white, while nr is a plain red disc.
