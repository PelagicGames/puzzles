Word Quiz

Can you name the word from its definition?

Play

How to play

  1. Read the dictionary definition. The word itself, and any words formed from it, are blanked out.
  2. The spaces show how many letters the word has. The tiles hold its letters plus some extras to throw you off.
  3. Click a tile to put it in the next space, drag it to a particular space, or type on your keyboard. Click, drag or press Backspace on your keyboard to send back a placed tile.
  4. When every space is filled the answer is checked. If it's wrong, try again.
  5. Stuck? Ask a friend for help. Solved it? Challenge them with the same word.

Definitions are adapted from Open English WordNet (CC BY 4.0), based on Princeton WordNet. See ATTRIBUTION.md for details. Progress is saved in this browser only.
